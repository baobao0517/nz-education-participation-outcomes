Five-year improvement, but uneven outcomes

The share of students attending more than 90% of Term 2 increased from 39.6% in 2022 to 64.3% in 2026. Yet 35.7% still did not meet this strict regular-attendance threshold; this does not mean those students never attended school.

Decision lens

Monitor sustained improvement across regions—not only the national headline. The 2025 reporting changes and annual source revisions should be considered when interpreting trends.

Decision support · Recommendations

Actionable investigation priorities.

Recommendations identify where to investigate and what to validate next. No district is labelled a policy success or failure on the strength of aggregate correlations.

01 · Priority support review

Gisborne + Northland

Both had regular attendance below NZ in each of 2022–2026 and NCEA L2+ below NZ in every year from 2019–2025, with substantial current gaps.

Evidence · 2025 NCEA gaps −9.3 pp / −5.7 pp; 2026 attendance gaps −13.0 pp / −11.9 pp.
Next evidence: school and locality patterns, transport / health access, whānau perspectives, early leaving and EQI mix; then co-design suitable support.
02 · Focused regional diagnosis

Waikato + Manawatū–Whanganui

Waikato has a smaller but persistent dual gap. Manawatū–Whanganui's 2026 attendance has caught up to NZ while its 2025 leaver outcomes still lag slightly.

Evidence · Waikato 5/5 attendance years below NZ and 7/7 outcome years below. Manawatū–Whanganui attendance 64.4% in 2026.
Next evidence: comparable school types and cohorts, school-level trends, attendance barriers, and later NCEA results; avoid inferring a 2026 outcome effect.
03 · Investigate a mismatch

Tasman

Attendance exceeded the 2025 NZ benchmark (+3.0 pp), but NCEA L2+ was lower (−5.0 pp). The region's attainment declined from 82.6% in 2019 to 72.8% in 2025.

Evidence · Moderate EQI group 72.9% L2+ (n=573) versus 80.1% in the 16-region same-group pool.
Next evidence: multi-year age-at-leaving patterns, schooling pathways, school composition, small-area volatility, and follow-up qualitative inquiry.
04 · Evaluate promising recovery

Taranaki + wider practice learning

Taranaki's NCEA L2+ rate rose from 72.9% (2023) to 79.6% (2025). This is a recovery signal, not evidence that any particular programme caused it.

Evidence · +6.7 pp over 2023–2025; 2025 attained level remains below its 2019 rate (81.0%).
Next evidence: programme timing, coverage, comparable schools and cohorts, implementation quality and independent evaluation before replication.
Interpretation boundary: Attendance data cover school students enrolled during Term 2; NCEA results cover the different population of school leavers. Geographic comparison does not establish that a change in attendance caused an improvement or decline in attainment. Observed differences can reflect participation, age and school composition, data completeness, changing reporting rules and educational pathways.
Transparency · Reproducibility

How the analysis was built.

The project is designed for auditability. It uses official public aggregate data, validates denominators and geographic coverage, preserves cohort differences, and documents the calculations in Python and SQL.

01 · Source & coverage

Official Education Counts datasets

Regular Attendance Term 2 regional time series (2022–2026) and School Leavers NCEA Level 2+ machine-readable records (2015–2025). All 16 Regional Councils are included.

Attendance source · Leaver source
02 · Validation

Counts before rates

Regional attendance numerators and denominators reconcile to official national counts (with Area Outside). School-leaver totals reconcile to 70,786 leavers and 55,062 Level 2+ attainments in 2025, including 6,045 leavers without regional attribution.

Official NZ attainment 77.8%; attributable-region pooled rate 81.1%, used only for like-for-like within-group diagnostics.
03 · Guardrails

Descriptive, not causal

Historical persistent-gap counts compare each region to the national rate from the same year. EQI is a school-level modelled group; ethnicity is total-response; cells with n<30 are suppressed in the subgroup explorer.

Changed 2025 attendance reporting codes and NCEA rules are documented. Do not interpret 2025/2026 time pairs as matched students.
04 · Reuse

Open methodology + reproducible scripts

Source CSV extracts, build scripts, validation report, SQL queries, and a short executive summary accompany the dashboard. The original official source ZIP is excluded from the public repository.
